## Approach

The problem asks for the average percentage of orange juice in a mixture of n drinks. Since equal volumes are taken from each drink, the final percentage is simply the arithmetic mean of the individual percentages. The time complexity is O(n) to sum the inputs, and space complexity is O(1) beyond input storage.
