# Watermelon — Accepted

Problem: https://codeforces.com/problemset/problem/4/A

Split into two positive even weights. The smallest valid total is 4; every even total at least 4 permits 2 and the remaining even weight. Check parity and exclude 2. Time O(1), auxiliary space O(1).

AI-assisted archived practice. Locally verified all weights 1–100 against enumerated positive partitions, including the sample 8, boundary 1, impossible 2, and maximum 100.
